Not Snap and not the Ubuntu archive, per D-021.

nginx compiles about thirty modules by default, including proxy, rewrite, gzip and the upstream balancers, so the proxy role needs nothing added for it. The six above are default-off. compat is the structural one: without it nginx refuses to load any third-party dynamic module, and adding one later would mean another build rather than a configuration change.
