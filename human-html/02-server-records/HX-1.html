Two observations came out of the same read, neither of them defects.

HX-1's upstream is the public ntp.ubuntu.com pool, which is the Ubuntu default rather than a chosen source. The whole fleet's time chain therefore ends outside the LAN. Time keeps working if that link drops - chrony holds the local clock and every member still agrees with HX-1 - but the chain is worth knowing when the fleet's time is questioned.

HX-1 spells its host principals HOST/, where the members spell theirs host/. Service principal names compare case-insensitively, so the two forms are the same principal. No action.
